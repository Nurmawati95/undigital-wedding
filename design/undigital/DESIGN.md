## Brand & Style

This design system defines the visual language for a premier Indonesian digital wedding invitation SaaS platform and interactive invitation viewer. The design marries the ceremonial intimacy, romance, and cultural weight of Indonesian weddings with modern tech-forward execution. 

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Ceremonial & Romantic:** Editorial typographic styling captures the emotional gravity of holy matrimony, cultural traditions (Akad, Pemberkatan, Resepsi), and bespoke storytelling.
- **Modern & Effortless:** Clean, breezy interfaces reassure couples and guests with high usability, lightning-fast interactions, and frictionless RSVP/gift management.
- **Cultured Sophistication:** Subtle, contemporary reinterpretations of traditional Indonesian geometric and floral filigree balance modern minimalism with celebratory warmth.

### Visual Aesthetic
The aesthetic bridges **Modern Editorial Romance** and **Soft SaaS Utilitarianism**:
- Luminous card-based interfaces with generous internal padding and deep roundedness.
- Polished, subtle glass and tonal surface elevations that feel light, airy, and ceremonial.
- Clear structural division between the couple's management dashboard (streamlined, conversion-driven) and the guest-facing interactive invitation experience (cinematic, personal, immersive).

## Colors

The palette relies on balanced layers of royal oceanic depth and airy celestial blues, creating an elevated celebratory tone.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Accent (`#2196F3`):** Reserved for primary interactive conversions—call-to-actions, RSVP submission triggers, interactive envelope opening stamps, and digital gift confirmations.
- **Deep Navy (`#0D47A1`):** The primary brand anchor. Used for primary narrative headings, formal dark cards, navigation headers, and authoritative UI states.
- **Soft Blue Surfaces (`#E3F2FD`):** Foundational canvas tint for alternate sections, nested cards, input fills, and RSVP response containers.
- **Decorative Accent & Borders (`#90CAF9`):** Subtle structural boundaries, active borders, status tags, countdown clock frames, and refined divider rules.
- **Base Canvas (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity primary background for readability and pristine card layouts.
- **Neutral Charcoal (`#0F172A`):** Text contrast tone for long-form narrative, wedding details, and guest notes, ensuring accessibility without harsh black tones.

### Functional Color Applications
- **Interactive States:** Hover states on primary elements step to `#1976D2`; focus rings utilize `#90CAF9` with an opacity glow.
- **Cultural/Ceremonial Highlights:** Gold/Bronze micro-accents (`#C5A059`) may be utilized sparingly for couple initials, wax-seal icons, or exclusive VIP badge markers.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy creates tension between ceremonial heritage and contemporary Indonesian digital design.

### Typographic Hierarchy & Pairing
- **Serif Lead (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for names of the bride and groom, formal Indonesian greetings ("Om Swastiastu", "Bismillahirrahmanirrahim", "Salam Sejahtera"), and ceremonial section titles. The italic variant (`title-script`) adds lyrical softness to quotes, love stories, and Quran/Bible verses.
- **Sans-Serif Engine (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Clean, highly legible sans-serif handling schedule details, banking/cashless gift data, guest forms, and dashboard metrics.
- **Micro-Labels (`label-ceremony`):** Set in all-caps with generous letter-spacing to announce ceremony milestones ("THE WEDDING OF", "AKAD NIKAH", "RESEPSI").

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a fluid 12-column grid system for builder and dashboard views, transitioning to an editorial single-column canvas (max-width `480px` centered) for mobile guest invitation experiences.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Viewport Rules
- **Desktop (min-width: 1024px):** 12-column grid, 24px gutters, 40px outer margins. Saas builder workspaces and templates use wide containers (up to 1280px).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid, 20px gutters, 32px outer margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with 16px gutters and 20px page margins.
- **Guest Invitation Viewport:** Standardized to a phone-frame wrapper (`max-width: 440px` or `480px`) on desktop viewports to simulate native mobile smartphone consumption, centered against a blurred photo canvas.

### Vertical Rhythm
Section blocks adhere to an 8px base rhythm. Invitation story segments utilize generous vertical margins (`space-xl` to `64px`) to create pacing between the couple's portraits, event details, live streaming modules, and greeting walls.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is soft, luminous, and multi-layered, avoiding harsh drop shadows in favor of ambient blue-tinted radiance and translucent materials.

### Tonal Hierarchy & Ambient Shadows
- **Level 1 (Card Baseline):** Subtle contrast using a soft ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(13, 71, 161, 0.05)`, edged with a 1px border of `#90CAF9` at 40% opacity.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating UI & Modals):** `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(13, 71, 161, 0.12)`, combined with a crisp 1px `#FFFFFF` highlight border. Used for digital angpau/envelope cards, song controls, and RSVP dialogs.
- **Level 3 (Cover Envelope / Sticky Navbars):** `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(13, 71, 161, 0.18)` for the opening invitation flap and RSVP bottom-action sheets.

### Glassmorphism & Overlays
- Floating music controllers, floating gift buttons, and mobile bottom navigations utilize a backdrop filter blur of `16px` combined with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` surface styling and a delicate `#90CAF9` border border.
- Modal backdrops use `#0D47A1` tinted at 40% opacity with `blur(8px)` to maintain brand presence behind alerts and banking popups.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates generous curvature (`roundedness: 3`, pill-scale) to project friendliness, organic warmth, and contemporary elegance.

### Shape Language & Geometry
- **Primary Cards & Containers:** Standardized on `rounded-2xl` (1.5rem) and `rounded-3xl` (2rem) corner radii to eliminate hard industrial corners.
- **Buttons & Tags:** Full pill format (`rounded-full`) for CTAs, guest category chips ("VIP", "Family", "Colleague"), and countdown timers.
- **Media Containers:** Couple portraits and gallery carousels use arched top profiles (`rounded-t-full`) echoing classical archways and modern Indonesian wedding architectural backdrops.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button (CTA):** Background `#2196F3`, text `#FFFFFF`, rounded pill shape (`rounded-full`), font `Plus Jakarta Sans` semi-bold. Padding `14px 28px`. Hover state shifts to `#1976D2` with a `0 8px 24px rgba(33, 150, 243, 0.35)` glow.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Transparent or `#E3F2FD` background with a 1.5px `#0D47A1` border and `#0D47A1` text. Used for secondary actions like "Salin Rekening" (Copy Account) and "Buka Peta" (Open Google Maps).
- **Ceremonial Open Button:** Prominent pill-shaped invitation trigger with subtle pulse animation and micro floral vector icons flanking the label "Buka Undangan".

### Cards
- **Soft Romantic Card:** Surface `#FFFFFF` layered over `#E3F2FD`, framed by a 1px border in `#90CAF9` (40% opacity), with `rounded-3xl` corners and 24px internal padding.
- **Ceremony Details Card (Dark Navy):** Surface `#0D47A1`, text `#FFFFFF`, headings in `Playfair Display` with `#90CAF9` accents. Employs a faint geometric floral pattern in the background at 5% opacity.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- **Input Fields (RSVP & Wishes):** Filled surfaces using `#E3F2FD` at 60% opacity with a 1px border in `#90CAF9`. Text color `#0F172A`, placeholder `#64748B`. Corner radius `rounded-xl`. Active focus ring switches to `#2196F3` with a 2px outer aura.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Attendance Selector:** Pill-shaped segmented controls ("Hadir", "Ragu-ragu", "Tidak Hadir") that highlight with `#2196F3` background and white text when active, rather than basic browser controls.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status & Category Badges:** Height 28px, `rounded-full`, padding `4px 12px`. Light variant uses `#E3F2FD` fill with `#0D47A1` text. VIP marker uses soft gold tint with deep navy typography.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Gift / Angpau Digital Block:** High-contrast card housing Indonesian payment gateways (BCA, Mandiri, BRI, QRIS). Contains a prominent one-tap copy button with instant feedback tooltip ("Nomor Rekening Berhasil Disalin").
- **Live Countdown Module:** Horizontal array of 4 pill-shaped or rounded-2xl tiles displaying Days, Hours, Minutes, Seconds. Soft blue `#E3F2FD` backgrounds with deep navy numeric figures in `Playfair Display`.
- **Wishes / Ucapan Stream:** Infinite or paginated comment cards with guest avatar initials, attendance confirmation pill badge, timestamp, and ceremonial blessing text.
